# About the Business

**Offer.** AI and non-AI automations for businesses, plus custom web-based systems. The flagship is a quoting + process-digitalization system built for a local print shop — it speeds up how fast employees draft quotes and digitalizes the workflow around it. The near-term play is to enhance/productize it and sell it to similar businesses. Adjacent services: web design (Claude, Higgsfield) and CRM/digital-marketing help.

**ICP.** Small/local, operations-heavy businesses — print shops and similar — that still quote manually and are ripe for digitalization. Plus anyone needing a custom web system, a website refresh, or CRM/marketing automation.

**Revenue model.** Early stage. One client (the print shop), cash only, no invoicing or accounting tool, no tracking — income is known roughly but unmanaged. Productizing the print-shop system is the path from one-off cash work to repeatable revenue.
