## What eats my week

1. **No cross-project visibility.** I run several projects at once with no set goals or visible steps — I can't tell when I'm advancing, and I have no signal for when to stop one and switch to another.
2. **Planning loops instead of execution.** I spend too much time debating across different Claude sessions *how* to handle things instead of actually doing the work.

These two are the reason this AIOS exists: a single operations center to set goals, track progress, and turn deliberation into shipped work.
